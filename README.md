## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am an **Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering** student at **Ramaiah Institute of Technology (MSRIT), Bengaluru** (*CGPA: 8.4 / 10.0*), driven by a deep passion for **Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence**, **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, and **Quantum Computing**. 

Currently, I am conducting research at top-tier institutions:
- 🔬 **Research Intern @ IISc Bengaluru**: Working on project **Prana**, an AI-powered personalized yoga and breathing guidance system using LLM architecture under faculty mentorship.
- 🌾 **AI/ML Research Intern @ Samagama (IIT Ropar)**: Developing computer vision diagnostic models for healthcare and PyTorch/Hugging Face ML pipelines for crop disease detection and yield forecasting in agriculture.
- ⚛️ **Quantum & Fundamental Science Explorer**: Authored an independent research article on quantum computing fundamentals and collaborated on astrophysics research examining universe origins.
